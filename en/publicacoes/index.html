Scientific articles

Datasets

  • Dataset · searchable on this site

    Catalogue of plant and fungal species of Cabeça do Cachorro (Upper Rio Negro)

    List of species recorded in the region, combining collections from the project's expeditions with records from scientific collections available through GBIF, with links to Flora e Funga do Brasil, POWO, GBIF and WFO.

Software

  • Web application · free to use

    Herbarium spreadsheet validator

    Validates, converts and annotates herbarium spreadsheets in the INPA/BRAHMS template, checking names against Flora e Funga do Brasil and geography against the IBGE database.
